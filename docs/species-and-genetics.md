# Species and genetics

The species decides **what** a colony produces. The Queen's genes decide **how well**. This page lists the species and
explains how to get them, and how research, the Genetic Laboratory, hybrids and traits work.

## Species

There are 59 species, and other mods add more (see [Compatibility](compatibility.md)). JEI, EMI and the Ant
Identification Book show what each one finds.

| Kind | Species | How you get them |
|---|---|---|
| Wild | Common, Wood, Stone, Snow, Desert, Farmer, Jungle, Swamp, Savanna, Shore, Blossom, Pale, Badlands, Army; Warped and Crimson in the Nether, Chorus in the End | Catch them with Tweezers in their biomes |
| Evolved | Farmer, Fire, the hunters, the Liquid Ants | [Feed](#evolving-by-feeding) the Formicarium |
| For fun | Cow, Chicken, Sheep, Pig, Rabbit, Baker, Bookworm, Party, Pirate, Fisher, Carpenter, Leafcutter, Weaver | [Feed](#ants-for-fun) the Formicarium |
| Mineral | Iron, Copper, Coal, Deepslate, Lapis, Amethyst, Redstone, Gold, Diamond, Emerald, Netherite, Soul, Ender | [Research](#genetic-research) them, then [mutate](#species-mutation) a Queen |
| Endgame | Wither, Dragon | [Research](#genetic-research) them, then [mutate](#species-mutation) a Queen |
| Hybrid | Nine crosses | Raise them in a [Queen Chamber](#hybrids) |

**Wild species** live where their name says. Shore Ants live on beaches, Blossom Ants in cherry groves and flower
meadows, Pale Ants in the pale garden, Army Ants in plains and savannas, Warped Ants in the Nether's warped forests,
Crimson Ants in its crimson forests (in nests of the Warped Ants' kind), and Chorus Ants on the End's outer islands.
Fire Ants and Soul Ants also live wild in Nether anthills.

**Hunters** are the only species that work at a Hunting Ground:

| Hunter | Comes from | Hunts |
|---|---|---|
| Army | Wild | Zombies, skeletons, spiders |
| Bullet | Jungle Ants fed gunpowder | Creepers, witches |
| Trap-Jaw | Swamp Ants fed slime balls | Slimes, drowned |
| Inferno | Fire Ants fed blaze powder | Blazes, magma cubes, wither skeletons, ghasts |

**Mineral and endgame species** follow one from another:

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Stone | Iron, Copper, Coal, Deepslate, Lapis, Amethyst |
| Iron | Redstone, Gold (Gold also from Desert) |
| Gold or Lapis | Diamond |
| Diamond | Emerald, Netherite |
| Fire | Soul |
| Amethyst or Chorus | Ender |
| Soul, with a Nether Star | Wither |
| Ender, with dragon's breath | Dragon |

**Hybrids:**

| Hybrid | Parents | Known for |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical Miner | Stone x Redstone | |
| Obsidian | Stone x Fire | |
| Crimson | Farmer x Fire, or caught wild in a crimson forest | Farms Nether fungus |

## Evolving by feeding

Farmer Ants (from Common), Fire Ants (from Desert or Warped), the hunters and the Liquid Ants evolve when their colony
is fed the right item. Put it in the Formicarium's feed slot. Each item is one attempt with a chance to work, and
after enough attempts it always works. The Formicarium's Info tab lists what the species can become and what that
takes.

### Ants for fun

Thirteen species that are not to be taken seriously. All are fed into being, and none is fed ant food, so feeding a colony
never changes it by accident. Each wears a costume of its own, on every ant of the colony: horns, patches and a bell;
a comb and tail feathers; a fleece; a snout, flop ears and a curly tail; long ears and a white tail; a chef's hat;
glasses and a pile of books; a party hat; a pirate hat and a striped shirt; a yellow rain hat, a coat and a fishing rod.

| Ant | Made from, fed | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cow | Army, milk buckets | Forages wheat. At a Hunting Ground it brings beef and leather. At a Liquid Chamber it gives Milk Droplets, which a Droplet Centrifuge spins into milk. |
| Chicken | Army, feathers | Forages eggs and seeds. At a Hunting Ground it brings chicken and feathers. |
| Sheep | Army, wool | Forages wool in the colours sheep come in. At a Hunting Ground it brings wool and mutton. |
| Pig | Army, carrots on sticks | Forages carrots, potatoes and beetroot. At a Hunting Ground it brings porkchops. |
| Rabbit | Army, rabbit hide | Forages carrots and dandelions. At a Hunting Ground it brings raw rabbit and hide, and from stage 3, rarely, a rabbit's foot (about two an hour). |
| Baker | Farmer, cake | Forages wheat, sugar and eggs. At a Harvesting Site it bakes bread, cookies, pumpkin pie and, at stage 3, a cake now and then. |
| Bookworm | Wood, books | Brings paper, books and bookshelves, and at stage 3 a Bottle o' Enchanting now and then. |
| Carpenter | Wood, crafting tables | Brings planks, sticks and ladders, later barrels and scaffolding. |
| Leafcutter | Farmer, shears | Brings leaves, moss and vines, and grows fungus at a Harvesting Site. |
| Weaver | Jungle, string | Brings string, leaves and vines, at stage 3 the odd cobweb. |
| Party | Common, firework rockets | Brings rockets, note blocks and cake, and at stage 3 the odd music disc. |
| Pirate | Shore, gold ingots | Brings sand and gold nuggets, at stage 3 nautilus shells and a rare emerald and, very rarely at stage 4, a Heart of the Sea. |
| Fisher | Shore, fishing rods | Brings cod and salmon, from stage 2 tropical fish and pufferfish, and now and then an old boot. |

The Cow Ant is the one species with three kinds of work. It is a hunter, so it does not farm.

## The Nether and the End

Some changes only happen in their dimension:

| Species | Where |
|---|---|
| Fire, Inferno | In a Formicarium standing in the Nether |
| Soul, Netherite, Wither | In a Genetic Laboratory standing in the Nether |
| Ender, Dragon | In a Genetic Laboratory standing in the End |

Take the Queen there (or the whole colony, in its Test Tubes) and bring her home afterwards: a changed colony lives
anywhere. In the wrong dimension nothing is used up, and the Formicarium or the laboratory says why.

## Genetic research

Mineral and endgame species are not fed into being. You discover them first, then make them.

**The scanner** is on the Genetic Laboratory's Research page. A scan takes 1,000 FE and needs no Queen.

| Put in the scanner | Result |
|---|---|
| A material | The right one reveals a species mutation (an iron ingot: the Iron Ant) or a gene (an iron block: Iron Jaws). The material is not used up. |
| Ants, eggs or larvae in a Test Tube, or a Genetic Sample | Records their species in the book |

- **Research is permanent and yours.** It is saved with the world and survives death and restarts. With
  [FTB Teams](compatibility.md#ftb-teams), a party shares its research.
- **Some research waits for earlier research**, such as netherite for the Diamond Ant. The laboratory says so.
- Catching ants and opening a Formicarium also record the species.

What one species mutation takes depends on its research tier:

| Tier | Species | A mutation takes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Iron, Copper, Coal, Deepslate | The material and 40,000 FE |
| Advanced | Gold, Redstone, Lapis, Amethyst, Diamond, Emerald, Soul | The material, 1 Evolution Catalyst and 120,000 FE |
| Experimental | Netherite, Ender, Wither, Dragon | The material, 2 Evolution Catalysts and 400,000 FE |

The material is a handful: eight ingots, six gems, four blocks or one Nether Star.

## The Genetic Laboratory

One block with six pages, named on the tabs across its top. The Queen sits in her Test Tube in the slot on the left on
every page. The laboratory lists only what you have discovered, and hovering a tab explains its page step by step. It
runs on FE: put a Generator next to it, or use any mod's cables.

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| Research | The scanner, and the list of what you have researched |
| Analysis | The Queen's genes with what each mutation does, and what her species can become: ready, still waiting, or evolved by feeding |
| Mutation | Turns the Queen into a researched species |
| Editing | Changes her genes (below). Needs no research. |
| Hybrids | The crosses whose two species you have met, with their chances. **Copy genes** turns an empty Test Tube into her Genetic Sample. |
| Stability | Locks traits and protects mutations for her daughters |

Editing has five operations:

| Operation | What it does | FE |
|---|---|---|
| Upgrade | Raises one trait by one level, for good. Level 8 takes Infernal Chitin, level 9 Void Chitin, level 10 a Nether Star. | 10,000 to 400,000 |
| Reroll | Rolls her traits again. Never lowers a trait and never takes one above level 6. | 40,000 |
| Mutate | Gives the mutation of the catalyst you put in | 80,000 |
| Purge | Removes a flaw | 60,000 |
| Copy genes | Makes a Genetic Sample | 10,000 |

FE is used only when the work finishes. Cancelling costs nothing.

## Species mutation

A mutation always works, and nothing can destroy the Queen. She comes out as the new species with her traits,
mutations, name and lineage unchanged. It needs:

- the **research**;
- a **Queen of the right species**, grown to the stage the change asks for: a Stone Ant Queen grown to stage 2 for
  Iron, a Gold Ant Queen grown to stage 3 for Diamond, stage 4 for the endgame species (her tooltip shows how far she
  has [grown](#a-queen-grows-with-her-colony));
- the laboratory in the [right dimension](#the-nether-and-the-end);
- the materials and the power.

**Taking a colony through it.** An empty Test Tube in the Formicarium's Queen slot takes the Queen out; her ants stay.
Mutate her and put her back. The colony keeps its ants, food, upgrades and history and becomes the new species at
once.

## Hybrids

A hybrid is the daughter of two species, raised in a Queen Chamber.

1. Put a Queen of one species in the laboratory with an empty Test Tube, open **Hybrids** and press **Copy genes**.
2. Put the Genetic Sample in one of the four sample slots of a Queen Chamber, in a colony of the other species.
3. Each daughter raised there may be the hybrid. Each new Queen uses up one sample, the left-most first.

The chamber says under its slots what the next sample will do: the hybrid and its chance, or that the two species make
no hybrid (the daughter then only mixes the two Queens' traits).

**The chance follows both Queens' traits.** No trait level is required, but better Queens cross more often. Each cross
has a listed chance and a trait level it is meant for. For Mechanical Miner Ants that is 30% at level 2.5:

| The two Queens' traits average | Chance |
|---|---|
| The cross's level (2.5) | The listed chance (30%) |
| Above it | 15% of the listed chance more per level (48% at 6.5) |
| Below it | Falls steeply, to a tenth of the listed chance with every trait at level 1 (3%) |

The result is multiplied by the two Queens' Mutation Probability trait, averaged over both.

## Traits

Every Queen has ten traits with levels 1 to 10. Each is a multiplier in the colony's simulation.

Worker Productivity, Gathering Efficiency, Food Efficiency, Egg Production, Larvae Growth, Hunting Strength, Farmer
Productivity, Mutation Probability, Environmental Resistance, Specialization.

- Wild Queens roll random levels.
- Daughters inherit their mother's traits with some variation, and may mutate.
- The laboratory's Upgrade raises one trait at a time, so every Queen can be perfected.

### A Queen grows with her colony

While her colony is at a higher stage than she has grown into, a Queen grows: into stage 2 after 10 minutes, stage 3
after 20 more, and stage 4 after 30 more. Each stage raises two of her traits, picked at random, by one level, never
past level 7. Higher levels are the laboratory's work.

She remembers how far she has grown, in her Test Tube too, so moving her to another colony doesn't grow her again. Her
tooltip and the Genetics tab show it.

The Formicarium's Overview tab shows both: the colony's stage and the Queen's own. While a new Queen is behind her
colony, a timer says how long until her next stage; hover it for the time of each stage on the way.

## Mutations

A Queen can carry any of 31 mutations. The Genetics tab, the laboratory and her tooltip say what each one does, such
as "+25% hunting" for Iron Jaws.

| Kind | How a Queen gets it |
|---|---|
| 20 genes | On purpose, in the laboratory, each from its own catalyst: Industrious from a redstone block, Keen Senses from a spyglass, Fertile from golden carrots, Lucky Strike from a rabbit's foot |
| 3 rare ones (Royal Vigor, Adaptive, Hive Mind) | Only at random, with a high Mutation Probability |
| 8 flaws (Sluggish, Gluttony, Weak Mandibles, Infertile and others) | Only at random. The laboratory's Purge removes them. |

A Queen carries at most 3 beneficial mutations. Flaws don't count.

## Stabilization

Stabilization is for breeding, and nothing needs it. It changes nothing in the Queen's own colony.

A daughter takes each trait from her mother or from the Genetic Sample crossed in, sometimes a level off, and a
sample's mutations pass on half the time. Stabilizing removes that chance:

| Stabilize | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Lock a trait | It passes on exactly as it is | 2 Royal Jelly, 30,000 FE |
| Protect a beneficial mutation | It always passes on when the Queen's genes are crossed in | 4 Royal Jelly, 60,000 FE |

The more of a Queen's genes are stabilized, the more often her daughters shed new flaws. Daughters start with nothing
stabilized, and releasing a gene is free.

## The Ant Identification Book

The book fills in as you discover things. Unknown entries show as question marks with a hint.

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| How to Play | The basics |
| Species | A page for every species you have met or made |
| Genetic Research | Scanned materials and what they unlock |
| Ant Compatibility | What the Queens of each species can become, and what that takes |
| Production | What each species brings in, and its Species Upgrades |

JEI and EMI show everything from the start.
